# Singapore: destination and visitor information

## Destination guide
Singapore is a compact city-state with an efficient public transport network. The MRT and buses connect the airport, central districts, museums, gardens and shopping areas. Check operating hours and ticket availability with each attraction before setting out. Gardens by the Bay, the National Gallery Singapore, the National Museum of Singapore and the Singapore Botanic Gardens are examples of visitor attractions; confirm current schedules and closures before visiting. Tropical conditions are hot and humid, with showers possible throughout the year. Carry water and plan indoor alternatives during heavy rain.

## Visa policy reference
Visa rules depend on passport nationality, purpose and length of stay. Singapore's Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) is the official source for entry requirements and the current visa-required country list. Visitors who need a visa must apply before travel through the channels specified by ICA. Entry permission is decided by Singapore authorities at the border; a visa does not guarantee entry. Check the ICA website for the latest rules before booking.

## Local travel notes
Follow posted rules in public places and use official transport and attraction notices for service changes. This guide is general planning information, not an official entry determination.
