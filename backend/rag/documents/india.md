# India: destination and visitor information

## Destination guide
India has varied regional climates, landscapes and transport systems. Confirm train, road and attraction schedules from official operators, and allow extra time for intercity travel. For outdoor sightseeing, use a destination-specific forecast and check local authority notices. Carry identification and follow local site rules.

## Visa policy reference
India's official Bureau of Immigration and Ministry of Home Affairs publish current visa and entry rules. Foreign nationals should use the official Indian visa portal for current categories and application instructions. Requirements depend on nationality, purpose and duration and may change; confirm the rules with official government sources before departure.

## Local travel notes
This guide is general destination information and does not replace official Indian immigration guidance.
