# Thailand: destination and visitor information

## Destination guide
Thailand offers urban, cultural, coastal and nature destinations. Bangkok has museums, markets, temples and indoor shopping areas; plan modest clothing for temples and check local access rules. Heat and seasonal rain can affect outdoor plans, so check local forecasts and official attraction notices. Use licensed transport and confirm current opening times and ticket requirements.

## Visa policy reference
Thailand entry conditions depend on nationality, travel purpose and length of stay and can change. The Thai Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the official Thai e-Visa portal publish visa categories, exemptions and application requirements. Travellers should verify their passport-specific conditions with those official sources before travel. A visa or visa exemption does not guarantee admission; border authorities make the final decision.

## Local travel notes
Respect local laws and customs, especially at religious sites. This guide is general planning information and is not a current official visa determination.
