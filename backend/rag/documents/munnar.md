# Munnar and Kerala: destination guide

Munnar is a hill destination in Kerala known for tea-growing landscapes, viewpoints and nature areas. Roads are winding and travel times can vary; arrange local transport with a reputable provider and check road conditions. Monsoon rain can bring slippery paths, reduced visibility or temporary access restrictions. Choose indoor or sheltered activities when weather is poor, and confirm opening hours and park access with local authorities before travelling. Kochi is a common gateway for onward road travel to Munnar.

## Entry and visa context
Munnar is within India. Foreign visitors must follow the visa and entry conditions set by India's Bureau of Immigration and Ministry of Home Affairs. Consult the official Indian visa portal for current passport-specific requirements.
